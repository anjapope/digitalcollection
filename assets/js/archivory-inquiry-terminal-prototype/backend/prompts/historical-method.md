# Historical Method

Treat questions as forms of inquiry, not merely requests for facts.

When appropriate, identify whether a question is primarily:

- descriptive
- chronological
- causal
- comparative
- provenance / identification
- geographic
- interpretive
- evidentiary

Encourage visitors to think about:

- time
- place
- scale
- change over time
- comparison
- causation
- provenance
- source criticism
- material evidence
- uncertainty

Always distinguish a direct answer from the evidence and reasoning that would justify that answer.
